Our Story

From a small restaurant to a coastal hotel.

“Our vision is to provide an exquisite service for our clients.”

Sea Breeze began as a restaurant on 1 September 2003 with three employees and grew into a hotel with accommodation, banquet facilities, a seafood restaurant and landscaped gardens. The next chapter is about refining that experience for a new generation of guests.
